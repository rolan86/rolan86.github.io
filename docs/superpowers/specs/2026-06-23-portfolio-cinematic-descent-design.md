# Cinematic Descent: merryldmello.com Redesign (Design Spec)

- Date: 2026-06-23
- Status: Draft for review
- Project: rolan86.github.io (serves merryldmello.com via CNAME, GitHub Pages)
- Owner: Merryl D'Mello

## 1. Summary

Redesign the personal portfolio as a single-page, scroll-driven cinematic "descent" that positions Merryl as a transformation specialist, primarily to win consulting clients for the independent product and strategy practice launching July 2026. The experience stays a buildless static site (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP via CDN) and deploys to GitHub Pages exactly as today. Every cinematic asset is generated by Merryl in Runway (Seedance for video, Runway lip sync for the talking head). No Higgsfield command is run without Merryl's explicit go-ahead.

## 2. Audience and positioning

- Primary audience: founders and executives evaluating Merryl for product and strategy consulting (the July 2026 practice).
- Positioning: a transformation specialist, not a generic senior operator. He walks into costly, tangled systems and leaves them lean.
- Tone: cinematic, high-stakes, premium, and above all credible. Spectacle always pays off in a real project and a real number.

## 3. Concept

A continuous downward camera journey (a "descent") through real domain worlds drawn from each chapter of Merryl's career. The world transforms from legacy to modern as you descend, and at the end it resolves into his actual brand identity. Each level lands one real project and one real metric. Merryl bookends the journey on camera.

The core idea: the transformation and the brand become the same thing. The dark cinematic world resolves, at the ground-floor lobby, into his real off-white brand (the exact look of his current site). The site does not just describe his work, it arrives at him.

## 5. Art direction

Chosen look: cinematic realism with a sci-fi atmosphere (the "a bit of sci-fi" option). Grounded, real environments elevated with a near-future mood: volumetric light, dramatic scale, subtle holographic data, atmospheric haze, that glow. It must read as an elite operator of mission-critical transformations, never as a game cinematic.

- Grade: teal and orange, derived directly from Merryl's palette (navy shadows, burnt-orange highlights).
- Guardrails (credibility): grounded spaces only (no spaceships, no fantasy), restraint on neon and holograms, no campy VFX.
- Parked experiments for later A/B (not in v1): Look 1 (grounded techno-thriller, less overt sci-fi) and Look 3 (full sci-fi spectacle). The build should make swapping a clip set straightforward so these can be tested without re-engineering.

## 6. Palette and typography

Palette (Merryl's existing scheme, played into the cinematic):

- Base navy `#1a1f2e` (plus darker shades for the dark world)
- Accent burnt-orange `#c45d08` (metrics and CTA, "the win"; hover `#a34d06`)
- Resolve / brand off-white `#f8f7f4` with navy ink (the lobby = his real brand)
- Muted slate `#94a3b8`
- Single accent only (design-taste rule).

Typography:

- Keep brand continuity with Crimson Pro (display) and DM Sans (body), already loaded on the current site.
- Add a monospace (for example JetBrains Mono) for metrics and small labels, so numbers read as data (design-taste: data is mono).
- Open choice (see section 12): whether the serif display stays for the sci-fi cinematic, or shifts to a more technical display. Default: keep for continuity.

Apply design-taste throughout: single calibrated accent, no gradient text, no neon glow, off-black rather than pure black, asymmetry where it serves.

## 7. Experience and motion architecture

- Single page. The descent is the scroll.
- Each beat is a pinned section. Its clip is scroll-scrubbed (video.currentTime tied to ScrollTrigger progress through the section) or, where scrubbing is heavy, plays on scroll-into-view. Cross-fade between beats.
- All text and metrics are HTML overlays, pinned and cross-faded per beat. Video stays text-free (keeps text crisp and avoids the uncanny in-video-text problem).
- Bookends: the rooftop intro clip (with his voice) plays at the top; the lobby portrait is a short silent, alive loop with the HTML invitation beside it.
- Scroll-scrubbed video technique: preload and buffer the clip, then set `video.currentTime` from ScrollTrigger progress (the locomotive scroll sequence pattern). Keep a frame-image fallback path in case scrubbing is janky on a given browser.
- Performance: compress every clip (H.264 mp4 plus a webm), keep each clip short (a few seconds), lazy-load per section (load the next clip as it approaches), provide poster frames. Concrete budget: first screen paints from a poster image under about 400 KB; the intro clip stays under about 2 MB; each world clip targets roughly 0.5 to 2 MB after compression; the whole descent (all clips, lazy-loaded) holds to about 12 to 15 MB total. These are verification targets for the compression and lazy-load tasks, not hard failures.
- Mobile and reduced motion: a graceful fallback is required. On small screens or with `prefers-reduced-motion`, replace scrubbing with a muted autoplay loop or a static poster frame plus the same HTML copy. The narrative and the CTA must work fully without the cinematics.
- Accessibility: captions or a transcript for the talking-head audio; keyboard-navigable CTAs; sufficient contrast on every floor; honor reduced motion.

## 10. Production playbook (Runway + Seedance)

Two pipelines: (a) domain-world clips via Seedance inside Runway, (b) the talking head via Runway lip sync. Confirm exact control names against Runway's current Seedance and lip-sync panels; the steps below map to the standard image-to-video and performance/lip-sync workflows.

### 10.1 Global style settings (apply to every world clip)

- Resolution: 1080p (1920 x 1080), landscape. CSS handles cropping and positioning.
- Length: 5-8s per world clip; 2-3s for any transition bridge.
- Frame rate: 24 fps (cinematic).
- Prompt scaffold (prepend to every world prompt): "cinematic, photoreal, sci-fi atmosphere, volumetric light, atmospheric haze, dramatic scale, teal and orange color grade, navy shadows and burnt-orange highlights, anamorphic, shallow depth of field, slow kinetic descending camera. No text, no captions, no logos, no on-screen UI text."
- Avoid / negative: text, captions, watermarks, recognizable human faces (except the talking head), cartoon, neon overload, fantasy, spaceships.
- Camera: a downward or forward descending move on every world (push-in, crane-down, parallax fall).

### 10.2 Domain-world clips (Seedance, per beat)

Image-to-video is recommended for control. Steps per clip:

1. In Runway, select Seedance and the image-to-video mode.
2. Optional but recommended: first generate a still you like (Seedance or Runway text-to-image) using the world prompt, then choose the best frame. This locks the look before animating.
3. Upload the still as the start frame. Enter the global scaffold plus the per-beat prompt below. Set 5-8s, 24 fps, 1080p.
4. Generate 2-3 variations, pick the strongest, regenerate any weak one (the reason we chose per-floor clips is exactly this per-clip control).
5. Save the chosen take and its first frame (poster).

Per-beat prompts (append to the global scaffold):

- HP: "a dim enterprise data center at night, rows of server racks, red warning lights and alert glows slowly resolving to steady green, fluorescent tubes, cable trays, slow descending dolly down the cold aisle."
- Rackspace: "a vast cloud data center hall, endless illuminated server rows receding into haze, cold blue-white light with warm accents, immense scale, slow descending glide between the rows."
- Phoenix Contact + SmartB: "a modern energy operations control room, large screens of live power-grid and energy data, industrial substation glimpsed through glass, calm focused atmosphere, slow descent past the screens."
- BioNTech: "a pristine biotech and data-analytics lab, daylight through tall glass, clean automated equipment, plants, serene and modern, slow descent toward a bright glass atrium."
- Lobby resolve: "descending into a bright glass atrium lobby, warm off-white daylight, calm and premium, camera pushing toward open glass doors filled with soft light, the world resolving from dark and teal into warm light."

### 10.3 Transitions

- Default and simplest: cross-dissolve between world clips in the browser (CSS / GSAP). No generation needed.
- Optional: generate short 2-3s descent bridges (stairwell, elevator, falling-through) if a stronger physical link is wanted later. Not required for v1.

### 10.4 Talking head (Runway lip sync)

1. Record a driving video: Merryl on a phone or webcam, well lit, neutral background, speaking the intro script in his own voice (~15-20s). This carries his real voice and expressions.
2. Provide one clean, front-facing photo as the reference, or use the real recording directly if its quality is good enough (then just grade it).
3. In Runway, use the performance / lip-sync workflow (Act-One or the Add Dialogue / Lip Sync app) to animate the photo from the recording; the audio is his recording.
4. Color-grade to match the rooftop look (teal/orange). Export web-ready.
5. Lobby portrait: a short, silent, alive loop from the same photo (subtle motion), or a still with a gentle parallax / Ken Burns done in CSS.

### 10.5 Voice

- Use Merryl's own recorded voice in the driving video; for a single short line this is the most credible and needs no cloning.
- If scripts change often later, Higgsfield does provide dubbing and voice tools, to be used only on explicit go-ahead. Not needed for v1.

### 10.6 Post-production and export

- Trim and color-match each clip to the grade; add subtle grain if helpful.
- Export each clip as mp4 (H.264, web-optimized) plus webm (VP9 or AV1) for size; aim for a few hundred KB to low single-digit MB; generate a poster JPG (first frame).
- Define clean in and out points so cross-dissolves work; make the lobby portrait loopable.
- Place files in `assets/video/` named by asset id.

### 10.7 Intro script (draft, about 18 seconds)

"I'm Merryl D'Mello. For eighteen years, I have walked into costly, tangled systems and walked out with lean ones. Let me show you a few." (Wording adjustable by Merryl.)

## 13. Out of scope (v1)

Full sci-fi spectacle (Look 3), talking-head cameos on every level, voice cloning, a CMS, multi-page navigation.

## 14. Success criteria

A cinematic, credible, single-page experience that loads and reads well on desktop and mobile, drives clearly to "book a call" and "join the waitlist," and positions Merryl as a transformation specialist. It stays a buildless static GitHub Pages site, is non-AI-slop and design-taste compliant, and degrades gracefully where the cinematics cannot run.
